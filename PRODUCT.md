# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Candidates preparing for a Next.js interview who need short, practical TypeScript and framework exercises. They work at a laptop, move between a study guide and VS Code, and need to see the next useful action immediately.

## Product Purpose

Next Interview Lab turns interview preparation into a sequence of focused coding missions. Success means a candidate can open an exercise, complete it without over-scaffolding, and explain the implementation aloud.

## Brand Personality

Sharp, playful, candid. It should make practice feel active and rewarding without becoming childish or distracting.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic neon-terminal styling, hacker-dashboard tropes, oversized motivational slogans, and decorative effects that compete with learning.

## Design Principles

1. Put the next coding action within immediate reach.
2. Reveal supporting guidance only when the candidate asks for it.
3. Make progress legible and satisfying, never performative.
4. Prefer confident editorial clarity over generic developer-tool styling.
5. Keep the study flow accessible, calm, and fast.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast, provide clear non-color state cues, keep controls keyboard accessible, and honor reduced-motion preferences.
